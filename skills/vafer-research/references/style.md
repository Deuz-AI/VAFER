# House style

The report is for a reader who has five minutes and no interest in how the research was done. Evidence and method go to evidence.md.

## Rules

1. **Answer first.** Open with 5–8 sentences that answer the question on their own. Then a "Bir bakışta" (at a glance) table: a timeline for history, a comparison for options, key figures for a market.
2. **Short, plain sentences.** Most sentences 25 words or fewer, one idea per paragraph, paragraphs of 2–4 sentences. Prefer everyday words to academic ones; explain a term once when it is needed.
3. **Name the source only when it matters.** Put the citation at the end of the sentence. Write "X'e göre" only when the attribution is part of the point, for example when sources disagree or a claim is doubtful.
4. **Tables for comparisons.** Three or more items on two or more dimensions go in a table.
5. **Foreign-language quotes.** Give the translation in the text and the original in a footnote. Quote only when the exact words matter; otherwise paraphrase and cite.
6. **Dates.** Write dates in the reader's calendar; give the original calendar form in parentheses when the source uses one: "1900 (Rumi 1316)".
7. **Uncertainty in plain words.** "Kesin değil", "kaynaklar ayrışıyor", "bulunamadı". Mark inference as inference ("muhtemelen", with the reason).
8. **Conflicts in one place.** In the body, give the verdict; put the dispute in the "Ayrışan kaynaklar" table: konu | A diyor | B diyor | karar | neden.
9. **No machinery.** No row states, tool names, check results, file names, "researcher", "wave" or English method words in report.md.
10. **Say each point once.** Cut repetition between the answer, the table and the sections.
