# Version 0.0.3 validation — 2026-10-26

**Acceptance was not established. The speed target was not established.**
“Ultra HQ” remains a target, not an earned rating. All available treatment reports
scored at least 95 under the preregistered rubric, but missing outcomes and timing
violations prevent the prescribed acceptance decision.

Implementation and instructions were frozen before the three new topics were
disclosed to the developer. One development/validation round was used. No product
or skill tuning followed the final exam. The original 267 Yalova experiment files
remain unchanged.

## Executable changes

- Answerable questions have provenance, required children and separate evidence,
  context, support and report-transfer declarations. These are not semantic proofs.
- Source-derived gap candidates are sealed before map comparison; every candidate
  needs a disposition. Scope narrowing retains reasons and history.
- ID-based patches preserve omitted records. Changed evidence invalidates dependent
  declarations; title-only edits cannot silently reconfirm invalidated tasks.
- Sequential batch operations reuse returned references and report the successful
  prefix on failure. They do not claim atomic rollback.
- Open-topic contextual reading is distinct from agreed whole-work reading.
  Existing action planning, capture diagnostics, search, incremental indexing and
  honest partial results remain available; old schema-1 workspaces remain readable.

All 113 offline tests passed in 42.330 seconds, including the previous 91 and 22 new
regressions. Skill frontmatter validation and an actual documentation-example run
also passed. None of these checks earns research-quality points.

The main skill shrank from 576 to 546 whitespace-separated words; the four edited
instruction documents together shrank from 6,386 to 4,731. A three-repeat local
transport check compared the same 8 ingests and 8 note commits: median 2617.96 ms for
16 CLI invocations versus 452.41 ms for one batch invocation. Logical operations
remain 16. This excludes model work, web time and research quality; OS caches were
not controlled.

## Prescribed research outcomes

Each arm had two fresh-context repeats per topic, the same inherited settings,
prompt and report format, 720 seconds including packaging, 16 web calls, 64 queries
and 80 other web suboperations. Neither research arm had child agents. Both could
retain notes/raw sources and performed source-driven self-audit. The development
Yalova trial separately exercised a fresh source-only examiner; its cost and
overrun are retained and are not a matched final-exam comparison.

Three fresh topic judges used the fixed 30/35/25/10 rubric, 20 items per report,
actual cited passages, and unchanged critical-error definitions. Scores were
frozen before the separate memory grading. Blinding was procedural; citation and
provenance style could disclose method cues. There was no independent human judge.

R01/R06/R12 retained partial material but no report or complete run log after a
session interruption. They were not replaced or assigned invented scores/times.
Only 5 of 12 assigned runs meet the measured research resource protocol. The nine
available reports received 180 fixed-item decisions and 169 paragraph-level source
audits; no critical error was found in these reports. These are bounded audits,
not a general accuracy percentage.

Treatment two-repeat means are 98.75 for history and 97.5 for science; the technical
mean is unavailable. No complete six-run overall mean or median is imputed.
Speed comparison required both arms to meet all quality/protocol gates and have
overall means within 5 points. That precondition was not met. The observed scores
and times do not establish general superiority.

Material deductions include mismatched local citation support for R02 and R04,
incomplete explanation of how chemical evidence constrains competing causes in
R04, and overstatement of an early hypothesis in R10. These findings were not
used to retune the frozen implementation.

## Retained evidence and limits

The separate retained-memory trial used twelve fresh responders, three previously
undisclosed questions each, 240 seconds and zero web access. All twelve responses
met those bounds. History and technical runs all scored 30/30; in science, R09
scored 28/30 for omitting the required Kigali/phasedown link and the other three
scored 30/30. The treatment mean is 29.67/30 and control 30/30, including the three
interrupted research snapshots. This does not establish a treatment memory
advantage and adds no points to report quality. The existing topic judges graded
memory against the frozen key, with their prior evaluation context retained;
provenance credit required rereading the cited saved files.

One metadata defect is disclosed: the original memory-prompt digest hashed an LF
string before Windows serialized CRLF bytes. Original manifests/prompts were not
rewritten. Canonical content matches both the recorded digest and the unchanged
preregistered questions; actual byte digests are separately recorded in the
memory ledger. No question content was changed.

The local, ignored `work/v003-2026-10-26/` directory preserves every assigned
outcome, raw source return, failure, report freeze, neutral judge copy, item score,
claim audit and surprise-memory response. Its `comparison.md` is the complete
Turkish report; `results.json`, `memory-summary.json` and `resource-summary.json`
are the machine-readable ledgers. These local artifacts are not required to
install the skill and are not automatically included in a repository clone.
